REFERENCE TRANSCRIPT — key excerpts
Source: podcast with a top short-form YouTube creator. Used to tune the
segmentation prompt and the ad/CTA guard. These are the excerpts that matter for
the pipeline's logic; the full episode is longer.

=== SUBSCRIBE ASK (must be EXCLUDED — Principle 4) ===
"Before we start this podcast I have one little favor to ask you. Can you please
hit the subscribe button down below so we can help more people every single week?
Thank you."

=== SPONSOR READ #1 (must be EXCLUDED — Principle 4) ===
"Do you want to grow and monetize your podcast but you don't know where to begin?
... That's where we come in. We've grown shows to over 100 million views ... If you
want to learn exactly our podcast growth flywheel ... schedule a call right down
below."

=== SPONSOR READ #2 (must be EXCLUDED — Principle 4) ===
"Do you want to know how we book the most amazing guests on our podcast? ... I've
put everything together in a simple step-by-step process. If you click the link
down below I'll give you the exact guide to book any guests on your podcast ..."

=== THE HOOK PRINCIPLE (the payoff moment — a GREAT clip) ===
"First things first, the best hooks don't have a hook. ... I used to think in my
videos I need to start by saying exactly what I'm going to do and then start the
video, but in actuality it's better to start the video immediately. ... With
podcast clips you don't want to be like 'okay I'm interviewing this person and I'm
about to ask them the craziest question' — no, just ask the question, just start
with the insane moment."

=== THE THREE-POINT HOOK CHECKLIST ===
"There's three things I check off: number one it needs to have SHOCK — ideally it's
visual because people see quicker than they hear ... number two it needs to set a
CLEAR EXPECTATION of what they are going to get ... and then give the viewer a
REASON TO WATCH UNTIL THE END — something that makes them so curious they can't
help but watch to hear how a problem gets resolved."

=== THE CURIOSITY FORMULA ===
"How would you make someone curious? You need to be specific as to what's going to
happen, but they don't know how that's going to happen."

=== ON REPURPOSING (edit as a native short) ===
"If you cut out all the fluff and it's literally edited to be as if it was a
standalone short, then I think it's totally fine ... reposting a raw clip would
probably flop."

=== ON TITLES / THUMBNAILS FOR SHORTS ===
"Thumbnails don't matter on shorts ... title to some extent does ... the key thing
for me is keeping it as short as possible ... while complementing the actual video."

=== ON THE COMMENT SECTION ===
"Almost every viral video has a strong comment section ... we want to be intentional
about designing our comment section — we want to know what we want people to say ...
spark people emotionally."

=== ON CONTROVERSY ===
"You can still spark controversy without being controversial ... just having an
unpopular opinion."
