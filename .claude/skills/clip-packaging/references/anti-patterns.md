# Anti-patterns — the channel's own floor, sorted into failure types

Source: the 80 lowest-view long-form clips older than three weeks, plus the bottom six of every archetype. Views in parentheses. Use this file in audit mode: name the failure type and cite the channel's own example.

## 1. Speaker's name + "Reveals / Explains / Describes / Reflects"
`Alex Hormozi Reveals The MOST Powerful Marketing Strategy in 2023` (63) · `Alex Hormozi Describes The Moment He Sold His Company for $46 Million` (69) · `Alex Hormozi Reveals Planet Fitness EVIL GENIUS Business Model` (78) · `Alex Hormozi Explains the Infinite Game of Business` (128) · `Alex Hormozi shares the #1 lesson he's learned from the wealthy` (115) · `Alex Hormozi Explains How to be Both Successful AND Happy` (258) · `Alex Hormozi on Staying Motivated Without Anger` (255)
**Why it fails:** third-person framing about the speaker; the viewer is not in it. Also a marker of the channel's pre-system era (ALL CAPS words, stale year, `#1`).
**Fix:** delete the name and the verb; what is left is the claim → make it a `You …` or `Why …` title.

## 2. Two-word virtue or abstract noun
`Take Responsibility` (225) · `Act with Urgency` · `Embrace uncertainty...` (242) · `Hard work is the goal` (54) · `The Overnight Success` (28) · `My Thoughts on Legacy` (10) · `Defining True Mental Toughness` · `Advice that has helped me the most` (29)
**Why it fails:** no situation, no number, no contradiction. Could sit on 500 clips.
**Fix:** find the one concrete line in the clip and title that.

## 3. Generic how-to with no qualifier
`How to Get More Customers` (92) · `How to get your first customer` (176) · `How to use AI to grow your business` (132) · `How to Raise Prices Based on Close Rate` (174) · `How to Build Unshakeable Fortitude in Your Business` (298)
**Why it fails:** search-query shape; nothing surprising.
**Fix:** add the qualifier that makes it a claim: *…People Will Actually Pay*, *…Without Ever Offering a Discount*, *…With 2 Questions*.

## 4. Memoir "How I …" about the past
`How I come up with content ideas` (65) · `How I Scaled My First Gyms` (199) · `How I Think About Investing in a Business` (182) · `How I Choose Which Podcasts to Go On` (250) · `How I Met My Wife` (301) · `How I Think About Growing on Social Media` (243)
**Why it fails:** about the speaker's history or process, not the viewer's future.
**Fix:** convert to hypothetical with a number: `How I'd …in 90 Days From Scratch` or `If I Wanted …, I'd …`.

## 5. Rare-niche hot-seat with no hook
`Helping a Pilot Scale His Aviation Safety Business` (289) · `Helping a Stem Cell Clinic Scale` (307) · `Helping an Accounting College in Brazil Scale` (364) · `Helping a Pet Care Coach Scale to $3M/Year` (356) · `Helping a Wildfire Sprinkler Company Scale to $200M` (404)
**Why it fails:** the viewer has to be in that niche to click. Even $200M doesn't save wildfire sprinklers.
**Fix:** lift the *universal problem* the owner has into a quoted question with a persona sentence, or lead with the number-gap (`$3M Consultant Stuck for 3 Years`).

## 6. Management advice as a verdict
`You Need to Hire Better Talent` (495) · `You Need to Learn How to Manage Your Time` (439) · `You Need to Focus` (1K on first upload) · `Never Underestimate Your Competition` (320) · `Don't Do Business With Convicted Felons` (400)
**Why it fails:** unarguably true and therefore unclickable. Nobody disagrees.
**Fix:** verdicts need a number, a timeframe, or a permission the viewer wants and does not expect (`You Don't Need a Job First`, `You Only Need Five Clients`).

## 7. Abstract philosophical question
`What is Learning?` (382) · `What Is Reciprocity?` (318) · `Did My Childhood Make Me the Way I Am?` (308) · `Do You Have to Go Through Hell to Become Successful?` (388) · `"What Happens If Your Ego Gets Too Big?"` (89) · `Is Who You Are Today Who You'll Always Be?` (1.5K)
**Why it fails:** no stake in money, time, or status; sounds like a seminar.
**Fix:** attach the question to a person and a consequence: `"I'm 37. Is It Too Late?"` (19K) is the same genre done right.

## 8. Speaker's biography question
`"How did you learn how to sell?"` (65) · `How I Met My Wife` (301) · `The Biggest Influence on My Life` (1.5K) · `Reflecting on the Tony Robbins Interview...` (72K — the exception, because Robbins is a name)
**Fix:** the viewer's version: `How to Learn Sales Without a Mentor`.

## 9. Trailing ellipsis as the whole hook
`If You Want to Be Exceptional...` (631) · `You're right...` (289) · `If I Died Tomorrow...` (489) · `I Lost Everything Twice... (Within 12 Months)` (285) · `Embrace uncertainty...` (242)
**Why it fails:** withholds the claim without stating a situation. 41 titles end in `...`; they sit below the channel median. The two that worked (`work 12 hours a day...` 72K, `Reflecting on the Tony Robbins Interview...` 72K) had a number or a name.
**Fix:** finish the sentence.

## 10. Too many ideas in one title
`I Don't Gain Confidence by Affirmations, Here's What Works Instead` (291) · `Why Determination Beats Brilliance in Entrepreneurship` (344) · `You're Selling What They Expect. Sell What They Hope For.` (465) · `Why You Don't Find Your Passion, You Create It` (605) · `You're Distracted by the Woman in the Red Dress (Business Lesson fr…` (453)
**Why it fails:** the payoff is already in the title; the thumb has nothing left, and the length passes 50 characters.
**Fix:** title = the situation or the first half; thumb = the second half.

## 11. The wrong archetype for the clip's content
`Helping an Agency Get More Clients` (20K, but the clip is about delivery capacity: "you're supply-constrained, not demand-constrained")
`"What Am I Doing Wrong?"` (29K) on a clip that is a structured talk about building an organic following.
**Why it matters:** these performed on brand strength; on a smaller channel a mismatched title kills retention and the algorithm stops pushing.
**Fix:** step 3 of the procedure — write down the core claim before choosing the archetype.

## 12. Thumb-text failures seen in the corpus
- Restating the title: `You Need to Sell Stories.` with a thumb *Sell Stories* would fail; the channel used *Nobody Buys Spreadsheets*.
- Topic labels: *MENTAL TOUGHNESS* (400), *MODELS* (33K but carried by title), *MARKETING BEATS* (truncated slab).
- Text over the whiteboard/frame noise so OCR (and the eye) reads *SCALING WORKSHOP* or *ACQUISITION.COM* instead of the caption.
- Five-plus-word clauses: *"Should I Quit or Push Through?"* as a thumb is a second title.
- Same number as the title.

## The one-line audit

For any existing package ask, in order:
1. Could this title sit on 50 other clips? (types 2, 3, 6)
2. Is the speaker the subject? (types 1, 4, 8)
3. Is there a number, a timeframe, or a contradiction? (if none → type 2 or 7)
4. Does the thumb share a content word with the title? (type 12)
5. Does the title already contain the payoff? (type 10)
6. Does the title describe what the clip actually argues? (type 11)
