# Rewrite drills — real transcript lines → the channel's title or thumb, with the move named

Every pair is a real MoreMozi clip. Read these when a rewrite feels flat. The "move" column is the reusable operation.

## A. Asked question → quoted title

| Views | Asked (transcript, trimmed) | Title | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 121K | "I've got a small YouTube channel, like really small, and I just don't know where to go other than just make more content." … "What do you sell?" "I don't have anything to sell." | "I Have a Small YouTube Channel, How Do I Make Money?" | Persona sentence + the question the speaker *answered*, not the one asked. | 39K | "Alex, how do I convince rich clients to hire me when my case studies are all from lower end small businesses?" | "How Do I Land Rich Clients With Small Case Studies?" | Compress 21 words → 9. *convince…to hire* → *Land*. *lower end small businesses* → *Small Case Studies*. Keep *Rich*. |
