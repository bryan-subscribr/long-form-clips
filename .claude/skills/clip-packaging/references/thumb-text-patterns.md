# Thumbnail text — the seven types, sourcing rules, pairing matrix, and the pixel spec

Measured on 779 MoreMozi thumbnails with designed text (OCR: macOS Vision), 756 of them with captions. Bold marks the yellow accent word where the original had one.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## The seven types

### 1. Verdict phrase — 34%
The speaker's conclusion rendered as a caption. Three or four words, sentence case, no quotes.

Price is the **signal** · Nobody Buys Spreadsheets · it compounds daily · Every Year **Compounds** · They **ghosted** me. · Cheap clients kill talent · No one **trusts** you · Others are already **profiting** · Real people lose **money** · Great leads, no deals · Winning gets boring too · Find one paying stranger · Everyone feels behind. · Nobody sees the grind · Intention decides everything · you're the bottleneck · Working hard, still stuck. · Losing the plot · The Shift Already Started · Days turn into weeks. · Punch above your class · Status games, not happiness · Burnout before breakthrough · Sell outcomes, not sessions

**How to write one:** find the sentence where the speaker lands the point, strip it to subject + verb + object, drop articles, keep the harshest noun. *"this is why price is such a strong signal for value"* → *Price is the signal*. *"you reward people for reaching out quickly by responding quickly… so you've trained your audience"* → *you trained them*.

### 2. One- or two-word label — 26%
The whole clip in one breath. Often with a period. Often ALL CAPS.

Just **Start.** · CLOSER. · Stop Waiting · Outwork Everyone · Let it burn · Do It Scared · MENTAL TOUGHNESS · Take The Shame · Suck out loud · name your price · Please Listen · GOALS HAVE A PRICE · Embrace the suck · Confront the work · Focus On You · Just say no · Grow your brand · Stop Networking

**How to write one:** it is either the command the speaker gives (*Just start. Let it burn. Say no.*) or the state the viewer is in (*Losing the plot. Famous but poor.*). If it needs the title to make sense, it fails.

### 3. Number / stat — 15%
A figure the speaker said, with just enough words to make it strange.

17 hour work days · 10,000x more input · 100 DMs a day · 150 DMS A DAY · $300 a month forever · 8 partners, 0 plan · YOU'RE 1 IN 25 · 5X LESS WORDS · $10K A DAY · $200K SKILL LEVEL · 71% IS YOUR SPOUSE · 90% beats 10% · 12x the work · $14K bought nothing · 1,600 products alone · 4X IN ONE YEAR · IT ONLY TAKES 7 DAYS · $4.5M and flat · 6K FOLLOWERS. MILLIONS. · 9 months straight · 50 IQ points smarter · 2 years for $30K · beat 95% today · $100 million regret · Zero cash, $20M sale · 8 HOURS NOT MONTHS · 100 DAYS · COMPOUNDS IN 6

**How to write one:** number first, then the noun that makes it a story. *"I could do 200 cold calls a day… define the input-output equation"* → the title took *200 Cold Calls a Day Isn't Leverage*, so the thumb took the *other* number: *10,000x more input*. When the title already has the number, the thumb takes a different one or a verdict.

### 4. Negation / contrast — 13%
Two halves that fight. `X but Y` · `X, not Y` · `all X, no Y` · `X. No Y.` · `more X, same Y`.

Famous but **poor** · All growth, no **profit** · More money, same **stress** · Two hands. **No** excuses. · Sell outcomes, not sessions · 8 HOURS NOT MONTHS · big revenue, no profit · No debt, no problem · No lies, no tricks · No stakes, no fans · Hire agents, not people · Stop learning. Start doing. · Not for you · One sale isn't enough · 50% markup isn't pricing · Great leads, no deals · Zero cash, $20M sale · VALUE OVER VOLUME · Status games, not happiness

**How to write one:** take the asker's situation and the missing thing (*revenue / profit*, *followers / money*, *growth / profit*), or the speaker's *not-this-but-that* (*outcomes not sessions*, *agents not people*). Commas allowed here only.

### 5. Raw quote in quotation marks — 5%
The asker's or speaker's literal words, when the words themselves are the hook. Curly quotes, lowercase allowed.

"I feel bad" · "i don't know" · "You're not gonna **die**" · "40% is too much" · "I am inevitable" · "Lost my **fiancée**" · "Only You Know" · "years of real work" · "13 people cancel" · "It's Just Jargon" · "Just volume, bro" · "So much life left" · "This is a scam" · "lost my biggest client" · "Now she's gone." · "Slept on the floor" · "Stop your ads!" · "It's just math" · "You must endure" · "Watch me suffer" · "Golden handcuffs." · "You're not Elon" · "I can't find good people.." · "I can't take any time off…"

**How to write one:** it must be verbatim or one word off. It is strongest when it is the *asker's* confession (*"Now she's gone."*, *"40% is too much"*) and the title is that same person's question — the pair reads like a diary entry and its headline.

### 6. Command — 4%
do it badly first. · Give them **three** prices · STEAL THIS SYSTEM · Charge your value · Stop underpricing yourself · Stop switching businesses · Fix Churn At Onboarding · Protect your leverage · Pick the easiest win · Price it 10X higher · START EXTREMELY NICHE · BE LIKE SMOKE · MASTER THIS FIRST · Stop Chasing Leads · Don't lose the spark

### 7. You-verdict — 2%
You sound **poor** · You're already behind · you're the bottleneck · You're hiding from customers · You're capping yourself · You want a hug · You might be wrong. · You never get it. · YOU'RE 1 IN 25

Rare because the title usually owns the "You". Use when the title is a quoted question and the speaker's answer is a diagnosis of the asker.

## Shape statistics

- Words: 1 → 17% · 2 → 13% · 3 → 29% · 4 → 37% · 5+ → 6%. Median 3 words, 17 characters.
- Case: 25% ALL CAPS · 13% lowercase start · rest Sentence case. Title Case is uncommon (feels like a second title).
- Period at the end: 5% — blunt verdicts only.
- Quotation marks: 5%.
- Overlap with title: 91% zero content words. Mean 4%.

## Where the text comes from (756 clips with captions)

| Source | Share | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Exact spoken phrase | 12% | *"I am inevitable"* at 68% · *BE LIKE SMOKE* at 28% · *Sleeping with my rep* at 0% |
| Two-word fragment of a line | 25% | *Price is the signal* ← "price is such a strong signal for value" · *Embrace the suck* ← "I have to suck for a period of time. And sucking sucks." |
| Key words of a spoken line | 35% | *17 hour work days* ← "repeatedly doing 16 17 a day of actual productive work" · *71% IS YOUR SPOUSE* ← "a 0.71 correlation to the quality of the relationship" |
| Tight paraphrase | 29% | *Nobody Buys Spreadsheets* (clip: "sell the story behind the jewelry") · *Famous but poor* (clip: 150K followers, broke) · *it compounds daily* · *Stop Waiting* |

Position of the source line: 39% in the first fifth, median 31%, upper quartile 62%. The payoff line does not have to be early — but it has to be there.

## Pairing matrix: title archetype → thumb type

| Title archetype | Thumb that pairs | Real pair |
|---|---|---|
| Quoted viewer question | speaker verdict, or asker's raw quote | `"How Do You Know How Wealthy Someone Really Is?"` → *You sound poor* · `"I Raised Prices 40% and Lost Clients. Did I Mess Up?"` → *"40% is too much"* |
| Helping a … | owner's confession, diagnosis, or number gap | `Helping a Home Services Owner Hire Faster` → *"I can't find good people.."* · `Boring B2B Beats a Sexy Consumer Brand` → *$4.5M and flat* |
| How to … | mechanism or number | `How to Find the Price People Will Actually Pay` → *Price is the signal* · `How to Reverse Engineer Any Skill You Want` → *100 DMs a day* |
| The X that Y | number or verdict | `The One Sentence That Shrinks Any Message by 5X` → *5X LESS WORDS* · `The Ruthless Focus That Got Me to My First Million` → *Just say no* |
| You [verdict] | proof number or sharper command | `You Can Have Everything You Want in 90 Days` → *17 hour work days* / *Just Start.* · `You Only Need Five Clients to Get Rich` → *Start absurdly expensive* |
| Why … | mechanism | `Why Most Ambitious People Waste Their 20s` → *Every Year Compounds* |
| If You…, Do This | the answer | `If You Sound Pushy When You Sell, Do This` → *BE LIKE SMOKE* · `If Your Calendar's Empty This Month, Here's the Fix` → *Stop Chasing Leads* |
| If I Wanted…, I'd… | the answer as a slab | `If I Wanted to Learn a Real Skill in 2026, I'd Do This` → *ONE SKILL ONLY* · `If I Were Paying Salespeople, I'd Do a 50/50 Split` → *$200K SKILL LEVEL* |
| Stop / Don't | the replacement | `Stop Selling by the Session. Start Selling the Outcome.` → *Sell outcomes, not sessions* |
| I / My story | the turn | `He Made $100 Million Trading. It Ruined His Life.` → *$100 million regret* |
| Compilation | the promise + `1 HOUR` badge | `How to Turn Cold Traffic Into Buyers \| 1 Hour on Advertising` → *stop guessing* |

Three pairing logics cover almost everything: **question → answer**, **claim → proof (number)**, **claim → sharper claim**. If your pair is none of these, it is probably a restatement.

## Design spec (measured on 773 thumbnails; pixel values at 1280×720)

| Element | Measurement | At 1280×720 |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical position | text block centre 15% from bottom; 82% of thumbs in the bottom third, 5% top | baseline of the last line ≈ y 640–660; block centre ≈ y 612 |
| Horizontal | centred 64% · left-anchored 52% (x0 < 8%) · right-heavy 10%; **all of the current top-12 are centred** | centre the block; `--align left` only for a deliberate old-style look |
| Lines | 1 line 79% · 2 lines 18% · 3+ 3% | wrap at ~26 characters |
| Line height | 12–17% of frame (median 15%) | cap height ≈ 85–120 px → font size ≈ 96–120 px Montserrat SemiBold |
| Width | median 68% of frame; 21% over 80% | text block 780–1020 px wide |
| Typeface | white geometric **bold/extra-bold** sans (re-checked 22 Sep 2026 on the top-12: SemiBold reads visibly thin beside the channel's) | Montserrat ExtraBold (vendored at `assets/fonts/Montserrat-ExtraBold.ttf`, OFL), fallback system Montserrat Bold / Poppins Bold / Arial Bold |
| Fill | pure white, **thin black stroke (≈4 px) + soft drop shadow**; the frame is *not* darkened on current uploads | stroke 4 px black, shadow offset 4/7 px blur 10; no gradient (`--darken 0`) |
| Line spacing | tight, lines almost touching | line height ≈ 1.0–1.05 × font size |
| Accent | one word yellow, 55% of text thumbs (71% of newest 400) | `#FEF731` (measured median) |
| Face | always visible, never covered | text sits below chin or beside the face; speaker usually right-of-centre or centred |
| Badge | red pill `⏱ 1 HOUR` with a white keyline, **top-right** on current uploads (older ones top-left), compilations only | ≈ 190×56 px at x 1280−40−w, y 40; white text on `#E53935` |
| Watermark | small source logo bottom-left on podcast clips | ≤ 4% frame height |
| Frame choice | the channel often ships mid-sentence frames; **for other speakers prefer composed: eyes to camera, mouth closed or mid-smile, face large, not mid-gesture** (a guest caught mid-word reads badly). Never the exact spoken second — scan ±10–15 s (`pick_thumb_frame.py` in the long-form-clips skill does this, with a reference frame so the host's close-ups are rejected) | close-up, single subject, text below the chin |

**Accent word rule:** the noun that carries the pain or the number; usually the last content word. *signal · poor · profit · compounds · ghosted · fiancée · die · $300 · 90% · three*. Two accents only in a contrast pair (*Two hands. **No** excuses.* keeps one).

**No-text fallback:** if the clip has no stinger line at all, a clean expressive frame is acceptable — the channel did it on 72% of its history — but since Aug 2026 it almost never does, and no-text thumbs earn ~55% of text thumbs at equal age.

Preview: `python3 scripts/thumb_preview.py frame.jpg "Famous but **poor**" -o out.jpg` renders the layout with these defaults (centred, ExtraBold 112 px, 4 px stroke, baseline 90%, no darkening; `--align left`, `--size`, `--stroke`, `--baseline`, `--darken`, `--badge "1 HOUR"` to override).

## Writing procedure

1. From the transcript, list every 1–5 word run that stands alone. Include the asker's lines. Typical yield 6–15.
2. Add the numbers the speaker said, each with the noun that makes it strange.
3. Add one invented contrast or opposite of the title's key noun (the *Nobody Buys Spreadsheets* move) — mark it as paraphrase.
4. Delete anything sharing a content word with the title you are pairing.
5. Rank: spoken verdict > contrast > hard number > raw quote > one-word label > command > you-verdict. Break ties by strangeness.
6. Pick the accent word.
7. Read it at 320 px wide (squint test). Cut a word if it does not survive.

## Anti-patterns in thumb text (seen in the channel's own noise)

- The title again, shorter: `You Need to Focus` → *Focus*. Fail.
- A topic label: *MARKETING*, *MENTAL TOUGHNESS*, *MODELS* — the corpus has these on low performers.
- More than four words of clause: *"Should I Quit or Push Through?"* as a thumb is a second title.
- Text over the face or over the whiteboard scribbles (OCR noise shows this happened on hot-seat frames).
- A number the title already used.
- Title Case captions — reads as a headline, not as speech.
