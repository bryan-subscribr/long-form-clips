# Scoring rubric — 100 points per package (title + thumb pair)

Score each of the 3×3 candidate pairs; ship the top three; recommend the top one. Ties break toward the thumb with the earlier spoken timestamp.

## A. Archetype ceiling — 25
Based on the archetype's median views on the channel, adjusted for whether the clip's format supports it.

| Points | Archetype (median) |
|---|---|
| 25 | `If I Wanted…, I'd…` (6,950) · `If You…, Do This / Here's What…` (5,500) — only if the clip really is advice to a situation |
| 20 | `You [verdict]` (4,000) with a number/timeframe/permission · Free-form claim with a person + number (case-study shape) |
| 16 | `How to … [surprising qualifier]` (2,700) · `I/My [event with number]` (2,750) · Free-form contrarian sentence (2,800) |
| 12 | Quoted viewer question with stake + number (2,400 median, but 121K ceiling; +4 if the persona sentence carries a number) |
| 10 | `The X that Y` (2,300) · `Why …` with absolute (2,000) |
| 6 | `Helping a …` with a hook (2,000) · `Stop/Don't` (1,900) · Compilation (2,250) |
| 3 | Unquoted question (1,600) · `Helping a …` generic · `How I …` memoir |
| 0 | Anything in anti-patterns 1, 2, 7, 8, 9 |

## B. Specificity — 20
| Points | Test |
|---|---|
| 20 | Could not sit on any other clip: contains a number *and* a niche/persona/contradiction (`"I Raised Prices 40% and Lost Clients. Did I Mess Up?"`) |
| 14 | One of: number, niche/persona, or contradiction (`Why Small Business Owners Always Undercharge`) |
| 7 | Specific topic but no anchor (`How to Win in Saturated Markets`) |
| 0 | Could sit on 50 clips (`How to Get More Customers`, `Take Responsibility`) |

## C. Thumb strength — 20
| Points | Test |
|---|---|
| 20 | ≤4 words, spoken in the clip (exact or fragment), has a hurt-noun or number, makes sense alone (*Sleeping with my rep* · *17 hour work days*) |
| 15 | ≤4 words, key words of a spoken line, makes sense alone (*Price is the signal*) |
| 10 | Tight paraphrase that is the opposite/consequence of the title's noun (*Nobody Buys Spreadsheets*) |
| 5 | Command or label that needs the title to land (*Stop Waiting*) |
| 0 | Topic label, restated title, 5+ word clause, or invented stat |

## D. Complement — 15
| Points | Relationship |
|---|---|
| 15 | question → answer, or claim → proof number, or claim → sharper claim, with zero content-word overlap |
| 8 | Complementary idea but shares one weak word (a verb form) — rewrite one side |
| 0 | Same idea twice, or any shared noun |

## E. Brevity and form — 10
| Points | Title |
|---|---|
| 10 | 30–48 chars, Title Case, straight quotes if quoted, correct number tokens, no banned words |
| 6 | 49–55 chars or one form slip |
| 2 | 56–60 chars or two slips |
| 0 | >60 chars, name in title, ALL CAPS word, stale year, trailing `...` |

## F. Deliverability — 10
| Points | Test |
|---|---|
| 10 | Title's core is spoken in the first 15% of the clip *and* the thumb's line is spoken before the two-thirds mark |
| 6 | Title's core spoken early; thumb is a paraphrase |
| 3 | Title is a promise the clip only implies (`…in 90 Days` on the Just Start clip) — allowed for verdict shells when the speaker's argument supports it |
| 0 | Title or thumb promises something the clip does not contain |

## Bands
- 85–100: ship.
- 70–84: ship if nothing better; note the weak dimension.
- 50–69: keep as Package 3 only if the other two are ≥70; otherwise rewrite.
- <50: discard.

## Worked scoring: Example 2 in `worked-examples.md`

`"How Do I Land Rich Clients With Small Case Studies?"` · *Punch above your class*
A 12 (+4 persona-less, so 12) · B 14 (contradiction: rich/small) · C 15 (fragment, alone-sense, no hurt-noun) · D 15 · E 6 (51 chars) · F 10 → **72**. Shipped by the channel; 4.3x average. The rubric says the title is fine and the ceiling is the archetype's; a `Why Rich Clients Say No (It's Not Your Case Studies)` variant scores A 10 · B 14 · C 20 (*"Don't pay me"*) · D 15 · E 6 · F 10 → **75**. Close call; ship the question because the clip *is* a question.
