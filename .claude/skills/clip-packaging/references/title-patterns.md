# Title patterns — the 14 archetypes, measured on @MoreMozi (2,795 clips)

Channel-wide: median 2,500 views, p90 17,000. Median title 43 chars / 8 words. 83% Title Case. Endings: `?` 161, `.` 121, `...` 41, `!` 0. Views are per-clip medians inside the archetype; "ceiling" is its best clip. Every example is a real upload with its view count.

Read the archetype, then its template, then the *what makes the good ones good* line. That line is the rule.

---

## 1. Quoted viewer question — `"How Do I …?"` (18.5%, median 2,400, ceiling 121K)

Floor: `"How did you learn how to sell?"` 65 · `"What Happens If Your Ego Gets Too Big?"` 89 · `"Why Can't I Say No to Bigger Opportunities?"` 86 · `"I Run My Parents' Business but Own 0%"` 110.

**What makes the good ones good:** a stake the viewer shares (money, time, fear), stated as a contradiction or a number. *Fully booked / barely profitable. $30M / unprofitable. Raised 40% / lost clients. 22 / start or work.* The floor titles are either about the speaker (how did *you* learn) or abstract (ego, saying no).

**Rewrite procedure:** take the asked question → strip context → first person → add the number or the contradiction from the asker's own words → one edge word if the answer earns it → ≤55 chars → straight quotes. Example: *"Do you think Gen Z are still competent enough for business or are reliable workers?"* → `"Is Gen Z Even Competent Enough to Hire?"`.

**Use when:** a real person asks; the question has a stake. **Don't when:** the question is about the speaker's biography or is abstract philosophy — use `Why …` or `The … That …` instead.

**Thumb pairing:** verdict in the speaker's voice (*You sound poor* · *nobody's watching* · *Punch above your class*) or the asker's raw line in quotes (*"40% is too much"* · *"Now she's gone."*).

## 7. Unquoted question (4.9%, median 1,600, ceiling 62K)

## Cross-archetype conventions

- **Length:** 30–40 chars median 2,800 · 40–50 median 2,500 · 50–60 median 2,200 · 60+ median 2,400. Aim 35–48.
- **Digits:** present in 25% of titles; median 3,000 vs 2,400. `$1M $10M $3M $100M $5M $100K` are the standard tokens. `$1M/Month`, `$3M/Year` for rates. `$2,000` only under $10K. `99%` is the channel's favourite percentage (23 uses), then `90%`, `10%`.
- **Timeframes:** `90 Days` (7), `1 Hour` (47, compilations), `8 Hours`, `28 Hours a Week`, `7 Years`, `6 Months`, `18 Minutes`. Digits, never words, except idioms (*One Hour, One Thing, Five Clients*).
- **Edge words** and how often the channel uses them: Actually 74 · Just 56 · Real 55 · Most 50 · Only 49 · Hard 38 · Rich 35 · Never 34 · Everything 30 · Still 27 · Anything 25 · Wrong 24 · Stuck 22 · Everyone 21 · Nobody 20 · Anyone 17 · Always 16 · Even 13 · Broke 12. *Secretly / Quietly / Shocking / Insane*: 0–1.
- **"Here's"** (32 titles, median 3,700) is the best closer: `…, Here's What I'd Do`, `…, Here's What to Fix First`, `…, Here's the Fix`.
- **Two sentences** (204 titles): first is persona or claim, second is question or consequence. Both Title Case, period between.
- **Parentheticals** (57): `(Do This Instead)`, `(and How to Fix It)`, `(You're Distracted)`, `(1 Hour Compilation)`. Payoff hints only.
- **Quotes:** straight `"` 475 vs curly `"` 43. Use straight.
- **Year:** `2026` appears in 41 titles, concentrated in `If I Wanted … in 2026` and `… Here's What I'd Do in 2026`. Use the *current* year only in those shells. A stale year (`… in 2023`) is a floor marker.
- **Never in a title:** the speaker's name (except compilations), `Reveals / Explains / Shares / Describes / Reflects`, ALL-CAPS words, `#1`, emoji, `!`.

## Title ↔ transcript facts (n=2,760)

- Title keywords spoken in the first 20 s: unquoted Q 41%, quoted Q 34%, statements 27%, Helping-a 16%.
- Title keywords spoken anywhere: 73–91%. Titles paraphrase; they do not invent.
- The line that best matches the title sits at a median of 2–7% into the clip; 64–76% of clips have it inside the first 15%. Cut the clip so the setup lands first.
- Quoted questions are rewritten, not transcribed: a 40-word rambling ask becomes an 8-word first-person line that keeps the stake and drops the context.

## Re-use is deliberate

108 titles used 2–7 times (235 clips). 73 groups are the same clip re-uploaded (same duration), 35 are a proven title on a new clip. `You Need to Focus` × 7 (1K → 28K). `You Can Have Everything You Want in 90 Days` × 3 (496K, 407K, then 104K as `If You Want Everything, Give It 90 Days`). `How I'd Make $100K in 90 Days From Scratch` × 2 (325K, 282K). Re-run a winner 1–3 months later with a different thumb line.
