# Speaker adaptation — what transfers, what recalibrates, with transformed examples

The MoreMozi system was measured on one speaker: famous, blunt, business-only, who earns absolutes. Three parts transfer unchanged; three parts recalibrate.

## Transfers unchanged
1. **The two-frame law.** Title = viewer situation, thumb = spoken payoff, zero overlap.
2. **The design system.** Bottom-anchored white semibold, 1–4 words, one yellow word (#FEF731), face visible, red `1 HOUR` badge on compilations.
3. **The cut.** Setup in the first 15%, payoff in the body, CTA appended.

## Recalibrates

### 1. Authority register → who is allowed to say "You"
Hormozi's `You [verdict]` titles (median 4,000, ceiling 496K) work because the audience already accepts his authority. For a speaker without that standing, a `You…` title reads as presumptuous and a blunt one-word thumb (*CLOSER.*) reads as arrogant.

| Speaker type | Title frame to lead with | Where the verdict goes |
|---|---|---|
| Established authority (Hormozi, Ramsey, Goggins) | `You …` · `If I Wanted …, I'd …` · `Why …` | title or thumb |
| Warm coach / therapist / teacher | Quoted viewer question · `If You're …, Here's What …` · `How to … [qualifier]` | thumb only, softened (*you're not behind* not *you sound poor*) |
| Analyst / educator (finance, science, history) | `The … That …` · `Why …` · `How [person] …` case study | thumb as number or fact (*71% IS YOUR SPOUSE* style) |
| Comedian / podcast host | Quoted line as title (`"…"`) · `I …` story | thumb as the punchline's setup, not the punchline |
| Athlete / performer | `How I …` memoir works here (the memoir is the product) · `The … Rule` | thumb as number (*5:30 AM test*) |

### 2. Numbers → only what the speaker says
Hormozi clips carry `$1M $10M $100M` naturally (12.6% of titles). A fitness speaker's tokens are reps, weeks, pounds, ages; a finance educator's are percentages and years; a therapist's are often none. Never import a dollar figure the speaker did not say. When there is no number, the specificity points come from the persona or the contradiction.

### 3. Edge words → match the speaker's vocabulary
Hormozi: *Actually, Just, Real, Most, Only, Hard, Rich, Never, Stuck, Broke*. A different speaker's edge words are in their transcript; count the adjectives they lean on across three clips and use those.

## Transformed examples (same clip idea, different speaker)

**Clip idea: "you need far less variety than you think; one message for 40 years" (the Dave Ramsey example in the jewelry clip)**
- Hormozi (as shipped): `You Need to Sell Stories.` · *Nobody Buys Spreadsheets*
- Warm creator coach: `"Do I Have to Post Something New Every Day?"` · *same message, 40 **years***
- Marketing analyst: `The 40-Year Rule Behind Dave Ramsey's Audience` · *repeat **yourself***
- Comedian/podcaster: `"I Said the Same Thing for 40 Years"` · *and they kept **tuning in***

**Clip idea: "if you can't sit still and focus for 8 hours, never expect to build anything great"**
- Hormozi (shipped, three versions): `How to Train Your Brain to Focus for 8 Hours` · *Embrace the suck* / `"Why Can't I Focus Long Enough to Get Anything Done?"` · *Sucking sucks.*
- Therapist: `"Why Can't I Focus for More Than 20 Minutes?"` · *you're not **broken*** (verdict softened, still speaker-frame)
- Productivity educator: `The 8-Hour Focus Test Most People Fail` · *one task. eight **hours.***
- Athlete: `I Trained My Brain Like a Muscle for 8 Hours a Day` · *reps, not **routine***

**Clip idea: hot-seat owner on Zoom 45 hours a week, only coach in the business**
- Hormozi system: `"I'm the Only Coach. How Do I Scale Without Burning Out?"` · *45 Zoom hours a week*
- Business podcast with a guest expert (less famous): `A Coach Doing $1.2M Alone Asked How to Scale. Here's the Answer.` · *45 hours on **Zoom*** — the title carries more because the speaker's name cannot
- Faith/ministry speaker: `"I Do Everything Myself. Is That Faithfulness or Fear?"` · *it's not up to **them***

**Clip idea: "in three generations everyone who judged you will be dead"**
- Hormozi system: `Why I Think About Death Every Day` · *an okay life*
- Stoic/philosophy channel: `The Three-Generation Rule for Ignoring Other People's Opinions` · *nobody will **remember***
- Comedian: `"Everyone Who Judged Me Will Be Dead Soon"` · *and so will **I***

## Multi-speaker channels (interview clipping)
- Title names the *idea*, not the guest, unless the guest is more famous than the channel (`Reflecting on the Tony Robbins Interview...` worked because of the name; `Dave Ramsey` in a pipe suffix worked once at 6K).
- Thumb text is the guest's line; a small guest-name label bottom-left replaces the podcast watermark.
- Compilations by guest: `[Guest]'s Best Advice on [Topic] | 1 Hour` only if the guest is a draw.

## Non-English or bilingual speakers
The archetype shells translate; the length rule does not (character counts differ). Keep to 7–9 words. Numbers and the yellow accent word carry across languages unchanged.

## Checklist before packaging a new speaker
1. Read three of their existing best clips' transcripts; list their edge words and number tokens.
2. Decide the register row in the table above.
3. Run the normal procedure; when the rubric's Archetype score points at `You…`, check the register first.
4. First ten packages: favour quoted viewer questions (they never presume authority) and put every verdict in the thumb.
